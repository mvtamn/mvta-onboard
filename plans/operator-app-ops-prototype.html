PROTOTYPE — demo data, not a real block

Operator counting during service

Try the three features Operations needs to decide on: an operator counting on an MVTA phone during service (phone mode), the screen locking while the bus moves (moving-bus lock), and the app working out which trip the operator is on (trip suggestion).

Drive the bus with the buttons in the middle, count on the phone on the left, and see on the right what OnBoard would record. The guided scenarios at the bottom run the awkward cases. The rules being tried: the phone locks above 5 mph and unlocks below 3 mph, each after 2 seconds at that speed; with no GPS it locks; a trip change is suggested and the operator confirms it.
