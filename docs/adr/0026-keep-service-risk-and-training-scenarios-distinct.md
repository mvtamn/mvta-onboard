# Keep Service Risk and Training Scenarios distinct

**Status:** accepted

Service Risk & Quality shows only live threshold breaches as Service risks; below-threshold observations remain Watch conditions, and repeatable demonstrations use explicitly labeled Training scenarios available to Service Risk viewers. Training never writes operational data, creates a Suggested Alert, or sends a message. Fixed-route Watch is a predicted departure delay from 10 through 15 minutes inclusive; on-demand Watch is a predicted total wait above 20 through its applicable standard, or an overdue request below its applicable standard. On-demand monitoring remains not connected until an approved source owner and contract, non-PII mapping, active operational zones, authenticated webhook, successful hourly reconciliation, and a live breach-to-Suggested-Alert test are verified. A failed 90-minute reconciliation enters Degraded feed: last-known risks remain visible, new Service-quality interventions are suspended, and an empty response must not imply no risk. Workspace health is shown locally as Loading, Authentication required, Not connected, Degraded, No active service, or Current; the application shell makes no cross-workspace live-data claim. Fixed-route data becomes stale after three missed five-minute polls; on-demand data becomes degraded after 90 minutes without authoritative reconciliation. Freshness derives from authoritative reconciliation, while webhook and vehicle activity are supporting evidence only. A Projected risk is visible immediately but creates one deduplicated internal Suggested Alert only after two consecutive authoritative updates; a Standard-exceeded or Critical on-demand request creates that draft immediately. Neither creates a rider communication or automatic publication. A recovered pending Projected risk is retained as resolved internal evidence. Publishers and administrators may explicitly resolve an open intervention; viewers may acknowledge or monitor it. Degradation preserves last-known records as read-only, disables intervention and alert-preparation actions only for the affected service, and does not impair the other service's current workflow. Reconciliation remains hourly. Fixed-route metrics name monitored routes and Watch conditions separately from routes affected and Service risks. Missing operational facts remain Unknown rather than being converted to a numerical or confidence claim.
