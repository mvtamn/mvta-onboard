# Separate assessment review and issuance

The person who reviews an Assessment Period may not issue its Final Assessment. An Assessment Reviewer establishes the evidentiary recommendation, while a different Issuing Authority accepts contractual responsibility for issuance; only an Emergency Separation Override authorized by a different administrator or executive may combine those duties, and self-authorization is prohibited. This adds operational friction but preserves a meaningful management control instead of reducing separate roles to labels one user can satisfy alone.
