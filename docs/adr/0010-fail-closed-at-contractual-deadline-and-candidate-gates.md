# Fail closed at contractual deadline and candidate gates

OnBoard may not share a Validation Draft or issue a Final Assessment unless its business-day calendar covers the required deadlines, and it may not finalize a period containing an unresolved in-term candidate. Early issuance additionally requires written contractor acceptance or a contractually permitted urgency reason. These gates may delay the workflow, but they prevent incomplete source processing or an invented deadline from silently becoming a contractual act.
