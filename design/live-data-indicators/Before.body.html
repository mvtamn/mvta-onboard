What the redesign fixes

  • A live feed and a dead feed differ by one 7px colour swap — #2fae66 to #8a1f1f. Nothing moves, nothing changes shape.
  • The blue accent banner reads as an informational note, not as a live connection; it uses the same tint as “preview data”.
  • Stale is stated in words but never in age — an operator cannot tell 40 seconds from 40 minutes without opening another page.
  • Four different treatments for one idea: a badge, a dot, a coloured row and a nav pill, none of them the same component.
