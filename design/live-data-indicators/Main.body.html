Pulse

One indicator for every surface that reports whether operational data is arriving. It pulses hard while the connection is open — twin halos, a comet on the countdown arc, a bloom at every arrival, a sweep across the banner — and then goes completely dead the moment the feed stops. The louder the live state, the harder a frozen one lands.

Anatomy
Core7–11px dot in --live-dot #2FAE66. At each arrival it punches to 2.1× and blooms outward — the loudest single frame in the console, and it fires only when data actually lands.
Countdown arcUnwinds over the module’s own refresh interval, with a lit comet running the leading edge. At a glance: how old is what I’m reading, and how long until it changes.
Breath haloTwo rings on a 2s cycle, offset by half a beat, so the pulse never fully rests. Present only while the socket is answering; its absence is the stale signal.
Freshness ticksThe last six polls as bars — filled arrived, faint missed — with a wave crossing them left to right, the direction time runs. Evidence that survives a screenshot, a colour-blind reader and reduced motion.
