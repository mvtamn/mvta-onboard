Motion

Every animation reports something

This is deliberately loud, and it still keeps the house rule: every loop below is driven by a real value — the poll interval, an arrival, a retry. The volume is spent on the two states that matter (data landed, feed alive) and nowhere else, which is what makes a dead indicator impossible to miss.

Rules the motion has to obey

  • Live and stale differ in shape (round core vs. squared, solid ring vs. dashed), in words and in colour — motion is the fourth cue, never the first.
  • Under prefers-reduced-motion every loop is switched off and the arc freezes mid-travel. Nothing above becomes ambiguous.
  • One loud element per region. A dashboard with three live feeds gets three small cores and exactly one banner wire — the volume is a page-level budget, not a per-component licence.
  • The arc is honest: if the refresh interval changes, the arc's duration changes with it. Never animate a countdown the page is not actually keeping.
  • Service runs 8am–10pm CST. Outside those hours an empty feed is healthy, so the overnight state is “live, no vehicles reporting” — not stale.
  • State changes are announced to aria-live="polite" with the age in words; the indicator carries role="status", as the current one does.
